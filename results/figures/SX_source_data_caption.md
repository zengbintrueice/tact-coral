**Supplementary Figure S-X | Architecture-independent transfer control: XGBoost thermal features.** (a,b) ROC-AUC and PR-AUC of three XGBoost regressors trained on SCS 2003–2010 and evaluated zero-shot on SCS test 2016–2019, GBR 2017–2020 and CARIB 2017–2020: FULL (13 dynamic channels), THERMAL (thermal-only: ssta, hotspot, d_hotspot_dt, hotspot_cumsum_4w, hotspot_cumsum_12w), NO-THERMAL (non-thermal channels). (c) CARIB discrimination (ROC-AUC and PR-AUC) across the three variants: the thermal-only variant retains most of the transfer skill, whereas removing thermal features lowers CARIB ROC-AUC markedly and PR-AUC by about half. (d) Marginal week-block bootstrap 95% CIs (1,000 resamples, seed 42) for ROC-AUC and PR-AUC in each domain: the NO-THERMAL interval lies below THERMAL and FULL in every domain and metric, while THERMAL and FULL overlap (CI separation is QC-checked at run time). (e) Cross-basin rank-association stability screen: per-channel Spearman rank correlation with y(t+4) in each basin; the physical anchor (PersA) and SST anomaly show the most stable cross-basin rank associations (star) while nonthermal channels are more basin-dependent.

XGBoost and TSCF share the same SCS→GBR/CARIB zero-shot transfer protocol: lead = 4 weeks, history = 24 weeks, train-only (SCS 2003–2010) scaler, τ = 0.90 pinned a priori; GBR and CARIB never participate in training, hyperparameter tuning or threshold selection. XGBoost predicts DHW (regression), so its scores never reach τ = 0.90 and the operational metrics (CSI/POD/F1) at τ = 0.90 are 0 or undefined in the source JSON; the comparison therefore uses ROC-AUC and PR-AUC. Because the XGBoost output is a continuous regression score and not a severe-event probability, probability-calibration metrics are not applicable; the source data report continuous MSE and a descriptive score-reliability statistic, neither of which is evidence of probability calibration. All values are read automatically from `results/xgb_transfer_control/metrics.json` and `results/metrics/metrics_v2.json`; none are hand-entered.
